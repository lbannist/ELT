# Robotics and Electro-Technologies 10: Course Outline

**CTS Courses Covered:** ELT1010 Electro-Assembly 1 | ELT1080 Control Systems 1 | ELT1130 Robotics 1 | ELT1140 Robotics Applications | ELT1910 ELT Project A

**Total:** 5 credits | 16 weeks | 80 class days (assumes 5 class days per week; adjust if your timetable differs)

**Source:** Alberta Education, Electro-Technologies Program of Studies (CTS, TMT)

---

## Big Ideas

1. **Circuits:** breadboarding sensors, inputs, and outputs
2. **Robots:** building and programming (mBot, VEX)
3. **Soldering:** wire, PCB, and prototype soldering

These foundations prepare students for 20-level sensors and control, and 30-level robotics, microprocessors, vision, and AI.

## Unit 1: Safety and Soldering (Weeks 1 to 2, 10 days)

**Focus:** Safe work practices, wire soldering, PCB practice, prototype soldering, cables and connectors.

**Assignments and activities**

- Safety Plan Sheet (hazards and controls, first aid, emergency plan, signed agreement)
- Equipment orientation: soldering iron, safety glasses, ventilation, fuse and protection devices
- Soldering Loops Assignment
- Desoldering
- Soldering a Board
- *New:* Cables and connectors task (e.g., make a power or audio lead with a connector; show coax, fibre, and telephone cables as examples)

**Outcome connections**

| Course | Outcomes |
|---|---|
| ELT1010 | 1, 2 (safety plan, hazards), 3 (fabrication techniques), 5 (cables, connectors), 6 (lab procedures) |

**Assessment ideas:** Signed safety plan, soldering skills checklist, quality of soldered boards, cables task.

## Unit 6: Robotic Challenge (Weeks 14 to 16, 15 days)

**Focus:** Student-led project applying circuits, soldering, control, and robotics. Delivered as **ELT1910: ELT Project A**.

**Challenge options:** a current VEX game (such as Squared Away or Rise Above) or an in-class challenge. Confirm that the challenge fits your hardware platform and the current season.

**ELT1910 requirements to build in**

- **Project proposal or plan:** team goal, deliverables, timeline, resources and materials, safety considerations
- **Connection to two or more CTS courses:** list which ELT1010, 1080, 1130, or 1140 outcomes the project uses
- **Assessment standards:** agree on success indicators with students before building
- **Work period:** build, test, and adjust; monitor progress against the plan
- **Presentation and demonstration:** show what was achieved and how it connects to the plan
- **Evaluation:** what processes worked, and what to improve next time
- **Career reflection:** complete the Career Reflection handout (ELT1910 Outcome 5; also meets ELT1080 Outcome 6)

**Assessment ideas:** Proposal, design notebook, challenge performance (for completion, not ranking), presentation, self-evaluation.
